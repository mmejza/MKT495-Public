PLNJ Thanksgiving Simulation

Case discussion and breakout activity replacement

Opens Wednesday, November 25 at 5:30 p.m. · Due and closes Monday, November 30 at 11:59 p.m. (Pacific time)

For Class #14, you’ll complete an individual simulation activity in WebCampus in place of our regular in-class case discussion and breakout group activity. In the simulation, you will advise Kathy Ayers, L&N’s VP of Marketing and Communications by making an initial decision recommendation, respond to new developments, and explain your final decision using evidence from the case and your simulation.

Completion of this simulation activity will earn participation credit for a regular class session. The activity consists of the following steps, which must be completed in the following order.

  1. Reading Check Quiz. This eight-question quiz tests your knowledge of facts and information given in the case. You will have unlimited attempts to correctly answer at least 7 out of 8 questions to gain access to the simulation. 
  2. PLNJ Thanksgiving Simulation. The simulation consists of three rounds. You must complete all three rounds, including the questions built into Rounds 1 and 2 and submit your final explanation in the app to obtain a copy of your completion receipt.
  3. Canvas Evidence Check. After completing the simulation, return to the Class 14 module and complete the four-question Simulation Evidence Check and correctly answer at least 3 out of 4 questions.
  4. Simulation Completion Submission. After completing the Simulation Evidence Check quiz, paste your simulation completion receipt into the Canvas assignment, include the line “I completed the PLNJ simulation myself,” and hit the submit button.

Plan Your Time

Set aside about an hour for the simulation activities, not including time for the case reading and the Canvas steps. You can consult the case during the simulation.
