Schedule, learning purposes, game mechanics, and decisions

Learning Purposes of Marketing Simulation

  • Mimic experience of implementing a marketing strategy.
  • Face challenges of adapting positioning tactics to multiple market segments
  • Make decisions based on analysis of performance results
  • Simulate customer acquisition and retention tactics
  • Experience trade offs between budget constraints and marketing objectives

Overview of the Game

  • Game progresses through 13 quarters.
  • You play the role of CEO of Marker Motion, Inc., a small manufacturer of medical sensors
    • $12 million annual revenues 
    • Sell directly to large customer segments 
    • Sell through distributors to small customer segment
  • You enter marketing decisions in each of the 13 quarters
  • You have an $800,000 budget to spend during the first two quarters. After that the budget size is determined by your company's financial performance. 
  • The simulation settings have an option to "fire" players (i.e., end play) for poor profit performance. (The "fire" option will be off during the Sept. 17th training runs.)
  • After completing 13 quarters, the simulation automatically calculates a total performance score that ranges from 0 to 100 points.
    • The score is a sum of weighted points based on performance in the following four areas, with performance weights shown in parentheses. 
      • Customer Satisfaction (30%
      • Market Share (15%)
      • Cumulative Revenue (25%)
      • Cumulative Profit (30%)
    • The table below illustrates how a point score is calculated.
