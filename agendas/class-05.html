Positioning: Hunley

Evaluate Hunley’s growth alternatives, present concise recommendations, and compare launch plans for different customers, prices, and channels.

Learning Goals

By the end of class, you should be able to:

  • Evaluate alternatives using explicit criteria.
  • Deliver a concise, evidence-based case recommendation.
  • Adapt positioning and launch decisions to different markets and channels.
  • Anticipate brand and channel-conflict risks.
