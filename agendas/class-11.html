Marketing Communications: Bring Shoppers to the Store with Purple in Mind

Purple wants digital ads that send customers into mattress stores already thinking about Purple. Today, your team plans that campaign: set objectives, choose target audiences, design the message, and decide where the ads run.

Learning Goals

  • Use the 6 M’s framework to structure a marketing communications campaign.
  • Set campaign objectives based on where customers are in the buying process.
  • Match target audiences, ad messages, and placements so each one supports the campaign objective.

Keep This Question in Mind

An ad can win attention online and still fail to bring anyone into a store. What would your campaign need to do so that a shopper walks into a store with Purple already on their list?
