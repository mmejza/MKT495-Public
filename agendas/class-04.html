Implementing Strategy

Use the marketing simulation to make integrated decisions, interpret market feedback, and improve strategy through disciplined experimentation.

Learning Goals

By the end of class, you should be able to:

  • Translate strategy into coordinated marketing decisions.
  • Interpret results without overreacting to a single metric.
  • Distinguish a useful experiment from an unsupported change.
  • Use evidence from one round to improve the next.
