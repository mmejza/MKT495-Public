MKT 495 • Marketing Strategy

Class 10 Agenda

Springfield Nor’easters • Case discussion

Price the Tickets. Fill the Ballpark. Cover the Costs.

A ticket plan can look good in a spreadsheet and still disappoint fans or lose money. Today, advise the Nor’easters on a first-season pricing plan that balances revenue, attendance, and affordable family entertainment. Which tradeoff are you willing to defend?

Learning Goals

  • Judge which survey findings offer a sound basis for pricing and where the forecast needs caution.
  • Connect ticket prices, package choices, attendance, and concession contribution to first-season profitability.
  • Defend a pricing recommendation, acknowledge its strongest counterargument, and identify evidence that would change your mind.

Bring a Recommendation Worth Debating

Keep this question in mind: Would you rather have a paid empty seat or an occupied seat sold at a discount? Decide what would have to be true for each answer to make sense.
