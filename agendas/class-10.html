Implementing Strategy: Team Simulation Play

Play the marketing simulation as a team. Agree on a strategy, make coordinated decisions each quarter, and use the results to adjust your plan.

Learning Goals

  • Agree on a shared strategy and translate it into coordinated marketing decisions.
  • Interpret quarterly results as a team and decide which changes the evidence supports.
  • Carry lessons from team play into your remaining practice runs and the final individual round.
