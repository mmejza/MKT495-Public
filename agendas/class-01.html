Class 1 Agenda

Wednesday, August 26, 2026 • 5:30–8:15 p.m. • Zoom

Course Overview & Semester Kickoff

Today we will establish how the course works, introduce the logic of marketing strategy planning, practice the structure of a case decision essay, and begin working together through discussion.

Learning Goals

By the end of class, you should be able to:

  • Explain the course’s principal learning activities and expectations.
  • Describe the basic logic of marketing strategy planning.
  • Recognize the required elements of a persuasive case decision essay.
  • Participate productively in a case-discussion group.
