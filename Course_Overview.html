MKT 495 • Marketing Strategy

Course Overview

What you will do, what you will need, and how your grade is determined

MKT 495 is an online, synchronous case-study course. You will learn to evaluate markets, customers, competitors, and strategic alternatives, then make and defend marketing recommendations. The course rewards consistent preparation, sound reasoning, clear communication, and active participation.

Start with the syllabus. Read the course syllabus carefully. It is the authoritative source for course policies, assignment requirements, due dates, and the class schedule.

Your Work in MKT 495

To succeed in this course, you are expected to:

Prepare
Read each assigned HBS Core Curriculum reading and case before class. Develop a recommendation and be ready to explain your reasoning.
Participate
Attend each Wednesday evening Zoom session and contribute constructively to breakout-group and full-class case discussions.
Apply
Complete five quizzes, eight case takeaways, two team case essays, the marketing simulation, a midterm exam, and a final exam.
Plan for steady work. Case preparation cannot be done well at the last minute. Begin readings and assignments early, keep pace with the schedule, and ask questions when concepts or expectations are unclear. No extra-credit assignments will be offered.

Technology Requirement

The five quizzes are administered at the start of designated class sessions using Respondus LockDown Browser. Install and test it before the first quiz.

Respondus setup instructions

Required Materials

Harvard Business School coursepack: HBS readings, case studies, and the online marketing simulation.

Purchase the HBS coursepack

You will need an HBS account to purchase and access the materials. Electronic course materials remain accessible through Harvard Business Publishing for six months from the purchase date and may be downloaded and printed as needed.

Optional: The coursepack includes Case Analysis Coach and Case Companion. These tutorials may help students who are new to case-study learning, but they are not required.
