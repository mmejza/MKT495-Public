Recommended Readings

Optional articles for extending and deepening your understanding of marketing strategy

The articles below are recommended but optional. They provide additional perspectives, examples, and frameworks related to the major topics covered in the course.

“Rethinking Marketing”
Roland T. Rust, Christine Moorman, & Gaurav Bhalla • Harvard Business Review, Vol. 88, Nos. 1–2 (Jan./Feb. 2010), pp. 94–101.
“Innovation Killers: How Financial Tools Destroy Your Capacity to Do New Things”
Clayton M. Christensen, Stephen P. Kaufman, & Willy Shih • Harvard Business Review, Vol. 86, No. 1 (Jan. 2008), pp. 98–105.
“The Elements of Value”
Eric Almquist, John Senior, & Nicholas Bloch • Harvard Business Review (Sept. 2016).
“Boosting Demand in the Experience Economy”
Harvard Business Review, Vol. 93, Nos. 1–2 (Jan./Feb. 2015), pp. 24–26.
“Competing on Social Purpose”

“Know Your Customers’ ‘Jobs to Be Done’”
Clayton M. Christensen, Taddy Hall, Karen Dillon, & David Duncan • Harvard Business Review (Sept. 2016).
