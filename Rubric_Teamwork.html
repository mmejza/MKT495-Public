Team Work Rubric

Standards for effective collaboration, contribution, and integrated team performance

Effective team work requires more than dividing tasks. Strong teams coordinate their work, collaborate professionally, and ensure that each member contributes reliably to a unified final product. Use the standards below to guide your team’s planning and execution.

Criterion Outstanding Acceptable Needs Improvement
Coordinate Team Actions Effectively

How well the team organizes, communicates, leads, and integrates its work.
  • Sets clear, high, and challenging performance expectations for team members.
  • Creatively structures roles and responsibilities to support the team’s work.
  • Practices participative leadership.
  • Communicates regularly about a wide range of issues related to the team’s work.
  • Produces seamlessly integrated outputs with consistent style, creative content, and a unified voice.
  • Sets reasonable performance expectations for team members.
  • Structures the team’s work logically.
  • Assigns or selects team leadership as needed.
  • Communicates regularly about the team’s work.
  • Produces outputs that are generally integrated, logically organized, and presented in a mostly unified voice.
  • Fails to establish clear expectations for team members.
  • Structures the work arbitrarily or not at all.
  • Avoids or hesitates to assume leadership responsibilities.
  • Communicates infrequently or inconsistently about the team’s work.
  • Produces outputs that feel fragmented, inconsistent, or like separate pieces assembled into one document.
Work Harmoniously Toward a Common Goal

How professionally and constructively team members collaborate with one another.
  • Acts in a highly professional manner—reliable, honest, respectful, and work-focused.
  • Anticipates potential conflict and agrees in advance on constructive methods for resolving it.
  • Makes consistently strong contributions to team efforts.
  • Actively seeks diverse and dissenting viewpoints.
  • Looks for opportunities to make the work easier and more effective for other team members.
  • Acts professionally toward other team members.
  • Uses constructive methods to resolve conflict when it arises.
  • Contributes reliably to team efforts.
  • Welcomes diverse and dissenting viewpoints.
  • Assists teammates when problems arise.
  • Acts unprofessionally or inconsistently toward other team members.
  • Fails to anticipate or constructively resolve team conflict.
  • Contributes inconsistently to team efforts.
  • Resists, dismisses, or feels threatened by dissenting viewpoints.
  • Provides help only when explicitly asked, or does so inconsistently.
Fulfill Individual Responsibilities

How reliably each member performs the duties expected by the team.

Each team member:

  • Produces more than the team expected.
  • Maximizes personal effort.
  • Works interdependently and assists others with their responsibilities whenever possible.

Each team member:

  • Produces what the team expected.
  • Provides sufficient effort to meet team needs.
  • Works interdependently with other team members.

Each team member:

  • Produces less than the team expected.
  • Could contribute more effort to meet team needs.
  • Works too independently from the rest of the team.
Remember: Strong team work is visible in both the process and the final product. A high-quality team submission should read, look, and feel like the work of one coordinated team—not several individual contributions placed side by side.
