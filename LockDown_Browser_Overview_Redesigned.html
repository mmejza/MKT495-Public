LockDown Browser & Monitor Overview

Setup requirements, practice instructions, and exam-day expectations

Respondus LockDown Browser and Monitor are required for designated online exams in this course. You must use a Windows or Mac computer, or a supported iPad.

Important: LockDown Browser and Monitor in WebCampus do not work with Chromebooks.

1. Install & Learn the Software

Before your first exam, review UNLV’s Respondus resources and install the software on the device you plan to use.

Do not click “Submit Quiz” until you have completed and reviewed all of your answers. Once you submit an exam, you will lose access to it and will not be able to reenter the attempt.

3. What LockDown Browser Does During an Exam

Calculator: Provides an on-screen calculator from the exam toolbar when enabled.
Full-screen mode: Displays the exam in a full-screen window that cannot be minimized.
Restricted navigation: Removes normal browser menus and toolbar options except those allowed by the exam settings.
Application blocking: Prevents access to other applications, including messaging, screen-sharing, virtual machines, and remote desktops.
No printing or screen capture: Disables printing and screenshot functions.
No copy/paste: Prevents copying material into or out of the exam.
Restricted shortcuts: Disables right-click menus, selected function keys, keyboard shortcuts, and task switching.
Controlled exit: Prevents you from leaving the exam until it is submitted for grading, except through approved exit procedures.
Best practice: Complete the practice quiz on the same device, network, webcam, and physical setup you plan to use for the actual exam. Resolve any technical problems before exam day.
