Task #1: 3C Positioning Analysis

According to the 3 C's model of positioning, a unique value proposition for a product must be relevant, resonant, and realistic for buyers, competitively distinctive, defensible, and durable, and feasible, favorable, and faithful to the company.

Customer Analysis Competitor Analysis Company Analysis

Resonant Defensible Favorable
Realistic Durable Faithful

Task #2: Segment Profitability

In the case, the Reserve card has been out for 11 months.

Assume the following for the Reserve card’s transactor, revolver, and dormant segments.

  • Transactors & Revolvers
    • Spend $16,000 per year on the card
    • % of spending carried on balance is 0% for Transactors, 50% for Revolvers
    • Renew card annually
  • Dormants
    • Spend just enough per year to earn the sign-on bonus
    • % of spending carried on balance is 0%
    • Do not renew card after one year

For all segments the interchange fee (1.5%), interest on balance carried (21%), annual fee ($450), acquisition cost ($350) are the same.

What is the profit per customer for each segment in the 11 months?

What do the profit per customers estimates imply about the Reserve card’s customer acquisition strategy?
