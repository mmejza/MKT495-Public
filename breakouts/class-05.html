MKT 495 • Class 5 Breakout Activity

Hunley

Case recommendation and two contrasting launch plans

Make a defensible case recommendation, then design launch plans for two sharply different opportunities. Record assumptions, decisions, risks, and budget allocations in the workbook.

Download the report-out workbook

1. Five-Minute Case Recommendation

  1. State the decision Hunley must make and the viable alternatives.
  2. Choose the criteria that should govern the decision.
  3. Recommend one course of action and support it with case evidence.
  4. Acknowledge the recommendation’s disadvantages and explain why you rejected the strongest alternative.
Time constraint: Build an argument that can be delivered in five minutes. Prioritize the logic that changes the decision.

2. Launch Grand Bassara Titaluk

Develop a plan for a premium rod with an $800 retail price. Specify:

  • Target customer and positioning
  • Primary message and proof points
  • Print-media choices and retail outlets
  • Major execution risks and responses

Allocate a $1,000,000 marketing budget across print, cooperative advertising, sponsorships, point-of-sale, search, and any justified additional activities.

3. Develop a Walmart Entry Rod

Develop a second plan for a $99 retail price and print-media support. Address:

  • Target customer, positioning, message, and media
  • The risk to the Klamath brand and how to contain it
  • The risk to existing retailer relationships and how to manage it
  • How the offer can be credible at the price point

Allocate a separate $1,000,000 marketing budget using the same categories. The workbook calculates the total and any amount unallocated or over budget.

Report-Out

Present your case recommendation, the decisive criterion, one important contrast between the two launch plans, and the risk that requires the most active management.
