Annie’s Growing Organically

Target-market choice and an integrated marketing mix

Use the case evidence to recommend a target market and build a coherent marketing mix. Record decisions and supporting evidence in the workbook; do not simply summarize the case.

Download the report-out workbook

1. Choose the Target Market

Compare Core Consumers and Primary Prospects. For each, identify:

  • Evidence of segment attractiveness and strategic fit
  • Likely barriers, risks, or uncertainties
  • Implications for Annie’s growth and brand
Decision: Recommend one primary target. State the decisive criterion and cite case evidence.

2. Develop the Product Strategy

  1. Identify the challenges Annie’s faces in entering frozen foods.
  2. Compare what consumers expect from frozen pizza with what they expect from shelf-stable products.
  3. Recommend Fully Organic or Made with Organic, explaining the tradeoffs.
  4. Choose the product variations Annie’s should launch and explain their fit with the target and brand.

3. Complete the Marketing Mix

Make specific, mutually reinforcing decisions for:

DecisionWhat to Specify
PricePrice point and rationale relative to the target and competition
DistributionPriority channels and retail placement
PromotionAdvertising spending, message, consumer promotion, and trade promotion

Conclude by explaining why the complete mix is internally consistent and where it remains vulnerable.

Report-Out

In two minutes, present your target choice, the most consequential marketing-mix decision, the evidence behind both, and one risk management should monitor.
