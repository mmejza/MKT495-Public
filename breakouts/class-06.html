MKT 495 • Class 6 Breakout Activity

Peak Sealing Technologies

Work with your assigned essay team to prepare a concise defense of your Peak Sealing Technologies recommendation. Team membership is unchanged from the original Hunley assignment.

Task

  1. State the central product decision and the viable alternatives.
  2. Identify the criteria that should govern the decision.
  3. Compare alternatives using relevant qualitative and quantitative case evidence. Make assumptions explicit.
  4. Recommend a course of action and explain why it is preferable to the strongest alternative.
  5. Address disadvantages, implementation priorities, and the most important risks.

Report-Out

Prepare a five-minute recommendation. State your decision, decisive criterion, strongest supporting evidence, and principal tradeoff. Be ready to answer questions and explain what evidence would change your recommendation.
