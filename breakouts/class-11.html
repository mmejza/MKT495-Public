Purple Innovation: Plan a Digital Ad Campaign

How can Purple’s digital ads drive customers to the store with a Purple mattress already in mind?

Your team is planning a digital ad campaign for Purple. Work through the campaign decisions in order: set objectives, define target audiences, design the message, and choose where the ads will run. Use the 6 M’s framework below to keep each decision connected to the others.

The 6 M’sQuestion the campaign answers
MissionWhat is the campaign’s objective or purpose?
MarketWho is the campaign intended to reach and affect?
MessageWhat content will be communicated?
MediaWhich communication outlets or vehicles will transmit the message?
MoneyHow much funding will be available for the campaign?
MeasurementHow will progress toward the objective be gauged and assessed?

Download the report-out workbook

Team output: One campaign plan covering Tasks 1–4, recorded in the report-out workbook. Pick a spokesperson to summarize your plan and take questions.

Task 1 — Set Campaign Objectives (Mission)

Marketing communications plans begin with an objective the campaign should accomplish: an effect on a target consumer’s behavior. For example, an advertising campaign objective might be to make 50% of the target market aware of a product’s brand name.

The diagram below shows the phases of a customer’s buying process, from realizing a need to using the product or service after purchase.

Using the diagram, identify three possible objectives for a digital ad campaign that best support Purple’s goal to “drive customers to the store with a Purple mattress already in mind.” As you identify objectives, consider where in the buying process your campaign’s consumers are:

  • Have they realized a need for a new mattress?
  • Are they already looking for a new mattress?
  • Do they already know the Purple brand name?
  • Would they put Purple on their “compare options” list?
  • Have they visited, or do they plan to visit, a mattress store?

Conclude: Choose two of your three objectives and carry them into Task 2.

Task 2 — Define Target Audiences (Market)

Digital platforms let advertisers target their ads at narrowly defined “micro-segments” of people.

For each of the two objectives you chose in Task 1, define a target audience. For each audience, answer:

  • Benefits or needs: What benefits will the audience want from a mattress?
  • Demographics: What are the audience’s demographics?
  • Lifestyle: What are the audience’s activities and interests?
  • Online shopping behavior: How does the audience behave online, e.g., page visits, video views, shopping cart activity?

Task 3 — Design the Ad Message (Message)

Designing an ad message means deciding what information the target audience should retain, who or what conveys it, what happens in the ad, and the ad format (text, image, video, etc.).

For each target audience you defined in Task 2, design a message for Purple’s digital ad that:

  • Aligns with the campaign objective (Task 1) and target audience (Task 2)
  • Reflects Purple’s value proposition
  • Promotes the advantages of visiting a physical mattress store
  • Uses a specific appeal (humor, fear, logic, sex, romance, personal, social, etc.)
  • Contains a catchy tagline or slogan

Then decide:

  • Format: What will the ad’s format be (e.g., video, still image with text)?
  • Endorsers: Will the ad use endorsers (celebrity or not) or online influencers? Who, and why?
  • Links: Will the ad contain links? To what (e.g., a product information page, promotional incentives)?

Task 4 — Choose Ad Placements (Media)

Where will the ads appear online? Be specific.

  • Meta platforms: e.g., Facebook right column, Messenger inbox, Instagram Explore. See Meta’s ad placement options for the full range.
  • Google Ads: search ads (text only)
  • Google Display Network: 2 million+ websites, videos, and apps
  • YouTube: In what type of video content would ads be most effective? Which placement type (e.g., skippable in-stream)? See YouTube’s ad placement options.
  • LinkedIn: see LinkedIn ad formats
  • X (formerly Twitter): see X ad formats
  • Specific websites: Which ones?

Should Purple also consider traditional advertising? Why, and which vehicles?

  • TV: What types of program content (e.g., drama or comedy series, sports events)?
  • Radio: What types of stations or programs?
  • Print: Newspapers, magazines, brochures?

Report-Out — Present the Campaign Plan

Use the Campaign Plan sheet of the workbook, which pulls together your work from the other two sheets. Summarize your plan for each of your two objectives:

  1. The objective and where it sits in the buying process
  2. The target audience it reaches
  3. The ad message, appeal, tagline, and format
  4. The placements you chose and why they fit the audience
