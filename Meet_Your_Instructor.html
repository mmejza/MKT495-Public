Meet Your Instructor

MKT 472: Marketing Strategy

Hello, I'm Dr. Michael Mejza

Welcome to MKT 472. I teach marketing strategy because I enjoy helping students learn how to think critically about markets, customers, competition, and business decisions.

My goal is not simply to help you memorize marketing concepts. My goal is to help you learn how to analyze marketing situations, interpret evidence, evaluate strategic alternatives, and make stronger recommendations.

A Note About MKT 472

Many students discover that MKT 472 is different from other marketing courses. The course emphasizes analysis, reasoning, and strategic decision making rather than memorization. If you consistently read the textbook, complete module activities in sequence, and keep pace with the course schedule, you will be well positioned to succeed.

What You Can Expect From Me

  • Well-organized course modules and learning activities.
  • Clear assignment instructions and grading expectations.
  • Timely responses to course-related questions.
  • Fair and consistent grading based on posted requirements.
  • Respectful interaction with students.

What I Expect From You

  • Read assigned textbook chapters before attempting module assessments.
  • Begin assignments early rather than waiting until deadlines are near.
  • Complete module items in the order presented in WebCampus.
  • Ask questions when expectations or concepts are unclear.
  • Participate professionally in discussions and course communications.
  • Take responsibility for keeping pace in a compressed five-week course.

Communication

Email is the preferred method of communication. To help ensure a timely response, include MKT 472 in the subject line and clearly describe your question or concern.

I normally respond to emails within 24 hours on weekdays and within 48 hours on weekends or university holidays. During periods of unusually high activity, responses may occasionally require additional time.

Please review the syllabus, module overview page, assignment instructions, grading rubric, and course announcements before emailing a question that may already be answered in WebCampus.

Meeting With Me

If a question cannot be resolved through email, I am happy to meet with students individually. Meetings can be held in person or through video chat and are arranged by appointment.
