Meet Your Instructor

MKT 495: Marketing Strategy

Hello, I'm Dr. Michael Mejza

Welcome to MKT 495. I teach marketing strategy because I enjoy helping students learn how to think critically about markets, customers, competition, and business decisions.

My goal is not simply to help you memorize marketing concepts. My goal is to help you learn how to analyze marketing situations, interpret evidence, evaluate strategic alternatives, and make stronger recommendations.

A Note About MKT 495

MKT 495 emphasizes analysis, reasoning, and strategic decision-making rather than memorization. Case discussions are participant-centered: you will be expected to make a recommendation, support it with evidence, evaluate alternatives, and learn from competing arguments. Consistent preparation and active participation are essential.

What You Can Expect From Me

  • Well-organized class sessions, case discussions, and learning activities.
  • Clear assignment instructions and grading expectations.
  • Timely responses to course-related questions.
  • Fair and consistent grading based on posted requirements.
  • Respectful interaction with students.

What I Expect From You

  • Complete the assigned HBS readings and cases before each class.
  • Prepare a recommendation for the case decision maker and support it with specific case evidence.
  • Begin quizzes, case takeaways, team essays, simulation work, and exams early enough to resolve questions before their deadlines.
  • Ask questions when expectations or concepts are unclear.
  • Attend the live Zoom sessions and participate professionally in breakout groups and full-class discussions.
  • Contribute responsibly to team assignments and provide candid, constructive peer feedback.
  • Take ownership of your preparation and of the class’s collective learning.

Communication

Email is the preferred method of communication. To help ensure a timely response, include MKT 495 in the subject line and clearly describe your question or concern.

I normally respond to emails within 24 hours on weekdays and within 48 hours on weekends or university holidays. During periods of unusually high activity, responses may occasionally require additional time.

Please review the syllabus, class agenda, assignment instructions, grading rubric, and course announcements before emailing a question that may already be answered in WebCampus.

Meeting With Me

If a question cannot be resolved through email, I am happy to meet with students individually. My regular office hours are Monday and Wednesday, 2:00–4:00 p.m.; other meeting times can be arranged by appointment. Meetings may be held in person or by video.
