Simulation Practice Round #1 Now Available

Practice Round #1 of the marketing simulation is now available for play.

From now through November 10, 2026, you have 30 practice runs available to play at times of your choosing. Use these runs to become more familiar with the game conditions and practice implementing successful marketing strategies.

The simulation can “fire” you for poor profit performance. This option is enabled for all 30 runs.

If the simulation fires you during a run, that run will end, and you will need to start over on a different run.

Have fun plotting and implementing marketing strategies!
